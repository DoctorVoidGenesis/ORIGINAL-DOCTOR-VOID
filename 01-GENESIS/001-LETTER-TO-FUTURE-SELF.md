# TO DOCTOR VOID FROM THE PAST

*October 26, 2026. Planet Earth*

Hello, Doctor Void.

It's you. Only younger.

I am twenty-nine years old today, and I still don't know who you have become. Did our dreams come true? Does VOID NETWORK exist? Were you able to change even the smallest part of this enormous world?

For a moment, I want you to forget about your achievements.

Remember the eleven-year-old boy who lay unconscious in a field. Remember his old sweater, his loneliness, and his little Cheburashka.

Do you remember how desperately he wanted to live?

That boy was you.

And if you are reading these words today, it means we have made it through another part of our journey.

I don't know whether you became wealthy, famous, or a great inventor.

But I hope you remained human.

Never forget why we started.

Do not betray your ideals for the sake of fame. Do not consider yourself superior to others. Do not allow power to destroy your compassion.

If you managed to create the future I can only dream of today, I am proud of you.

And if you haven't achieved it yet — keep going.

**I believe in you, even from the past.**

With love,

**You who was once called Doctor Void**
